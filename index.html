Civic Tech & DPI

Beyond myScheme: Proactive Entitlement Engine

The Recommendation: Move from manual questionnaires to an auto-entitlement engine. Secure identity authentication cross-queries verified registries (land, tax, ration) to show what you qualify for with zero paperwork. Includes a 1-click Claim Discrepancy button with a 15-day SLA review, and a universal GiveItUp switch to exit subsidies as income grows.

🌐 Global Benchmark: Estonia’s proactive X-Road state meets India Stack.

💡 Optimist Take: If shopping apps know our shoe size instantly, public portals can certainly know if we qualify for a farmer stipend.
Civic Tech

The "Unified Digging" Ledger

The Recommendation: A shared open-API coordinate portal for PWD, water boards, electricity, and broadband telcos. If a trench is dug, all 4 utilities get a 14-day synchronised window before a 24-month "no-dig" guarantee locks in.

🌐 Global Benchmark: Singapore's OneService Infrastructure utility routing model.

🚜 Optimist Take: Roads are meant for wheels, not for being treated like an archaeological excavation site every Tuesday.
Civic Ecology

Citizen-Adopted Urban Tree QR Tags

The Recommendation: Attach durable QR bark collars to municipal saplings. Local school kids and shopkeepers scan weekly to record watering and health. Gamified dashboards rank wards by tree survival rates, not just trees planted.

🌐 Global Benchmark: City of Melbourne's interactive "Email a Tree" & Urban Forest portal.

🌳 Optimist Take: Instead of planting 10,000 trees that vanish, let's keep 500 alive and celebrate their birthdays.
Everyday Ease

UPI-Enabled Public Water & Clean Restrooms

The Recommendation: ₹1 frictionless micro-UPI tap for self-cleaning urban water dispensers and automated public washroom turns, funding immediate housekeeping staff via transparent direct-escrow deposits.

🌐 Global Benchmark: Tokyo Metro Tokyo-Toilet Project meets India's world-leading UPI stack.

🚰 Optimist Take: If we can buy a ₹5 elaichi by scanning a phone, our public utilities should be just as smooth.
